# Codex Research Governance Rules

Codex should use `AGENTS.md` as its thin entry point and the shared documents
under `docs/` as the source of truth.

Before formal experiments, Codex must check:

- `docs/RESEARCH_CONTRACT.md`
- `docs/DATA_PROTOCOL.md`
- `docs/METRIC_CONTRACT.md`
- `docs/EXPERIMENT_POLICY.md`
- active experiment spec

Do not promote pilot results or Shape-200 results to final long-term ECG
claims.
