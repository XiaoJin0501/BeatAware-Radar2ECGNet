# Paper Claim Audit

Use before drafting or revising manuscript result claims.

Scan for:

- numeric results;
- improvement claims;
- causal language;
- statistical significance;
- dataset/protocol descriptions;
- baseline descriptions;
- Shape-200 vs long-term ECG wording.

Map each claim to `docs/PAPER_CLAIM_LEDGER.md` and formal manifests. Flag
unsupported claims and adapted baselines described as official originals.
