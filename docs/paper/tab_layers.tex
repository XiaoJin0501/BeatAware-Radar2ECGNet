\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\scriptsize
\item All convolutions have stride 1; stem convolutions have padding $(1,1)$ and dilation 1; head convolutions have padding 2 and dilation 1. TCN padding is $3d$ (noncausal, length preserving). Convolutions preceding BN have no bias; final head convolutions have bias. TCN residuals are block-local, with no extra global skip.
\item RF $=1+2(7-1)\sum d=373$ latent positions at nominal 50 Hz ($400/8$), i.e., $7.46$ s; this is the TCN convolutional RF, not an exact raw-input RF including the stem/interpolation. Its first-to-last position span is $372/50=7.44$ s.
\item $a_c$: \texttt{align\_corners}; sg: stop-gradient. Phase grid $t_i=a+(i/199)(b-a-1)$, $i=0,\ldots,199$, uses detached predicted R bounds $[a,b)$ at inference. Sampling uses bilinear \texttt{grid\_sample}, border padding, $a_c=\mathrm{True}$. No interpolation layer precedes the shape-head convolutions after concatenation.
\item Event training uses logits with binary cross-entropy-with-logits; Sigmoid supplies probabilities for decoding/conditioning. Shape Sigmoid is applied after the final convolution. For CR\_RVS, input is $B\times1\times65\times93$; frequency pooling gives $65\to32\to1$, then temporal interpolation $93\to400$. Subsequent shapes are identical; its trainable parameter count is $1{,}424{,}581$.
\end{tablenotes}
